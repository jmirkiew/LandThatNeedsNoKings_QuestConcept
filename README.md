“Land That Needs No Kings” is a quest concept for The Witcher 3 .A local priest hires Geralt to investigate a remote, all-female village. As Geralt uncovers a community bound by shared trauma and fiercely protective sisterhood, he realizes their peaceful sanctuary hides a dark, ancient bargain rooted deep within the woods.

Geralt is stuck with a brutal choice: let the women keep their sanctuary by turning a blind eye to their creepy pact with an ancient monster, or put a stop it and destroy the only safe haven these women have left.

The quest focuses on emotional weight of decisions and an Empathy vs Reason dynamic.
